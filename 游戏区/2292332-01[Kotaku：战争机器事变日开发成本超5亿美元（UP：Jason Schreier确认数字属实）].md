《战争机器：E-Day》是一款极棒的游戏，其Metacritic评分是自原版Xbox 360三部曲以来该系列所有作品中最高的。但早期估算表明，这种正面热度并未转化为大片级的销量。这使得它成为Xbox回归独占策略的一个有趣测试案例，尤其是对于一款成本如此之高的游戏而言。

关于《战争机器：E-Day》预算的最早传闻出现在6月，当时该作在Xbox夏季展示会上亮相。Insider Gaming的Tom Henderson当时表示，他听说这款射击游戏的发售成本“高得离谱”，超过4亿美元。Windows Central的Jez Corden反驳了这一说法。他暗示预算更低，并指出由于The Coalition工作室为Xbox Game Studios旗下各团队提供支持性工作，核算其成本并不容易。

但据一位熟悉Xbox业务的消息人士称，《战争机器：E-Day》的成本实际上要高得多。他们告诉Kotaku，成本超过5亿美元，而且这还不包括营销费用。这将使其显著高于本世代其他主机独占大作的已报道预算，比如《漫威蜘蛛侠2》。E-Day需要在所有平台上卖出数百万份才能弥补差额。微软未回应置评请求。

Gears of War: E-Day is a terrific game and it has the highest Metacritic score of any entry in the franchise since the original Xbox 360 trilogy. But early estimates suggest the positive buzz has not resulted in blockbuster sales numbers. That makes it an interesting test case for Xbox's return to exclusivity, especially for a game that cost so much.

The first rumors of Gears of War: E-Day's budget arose in June after it was shown during Xbox's summer showcase. Insider Gaming's Tom Henderson said at the time he'd heard the cost to ship the shooter was "insane" and over $400 million. Windows Central's Jez Corden pushed back on that claim. He suggested the budget was lower and that accounting for the cost of The Coalition is tricky because of the work the studio does supporting teams across Xbox Game Studios.

But according to one source familiar with Xbox's business, the cost of Gears of War: E-Day is in fact much higher. They told Kotaku it was over $500 million, and that's before you include marketing. That would put it significantly above the reported budgets of other console-exclusive blockbusters this generation, like Marvel's Spider-Man 2. E-Day would need to sell millions and millions of copies across all platforms to make up the difference. Microsoft did not respond to a request for comment.
